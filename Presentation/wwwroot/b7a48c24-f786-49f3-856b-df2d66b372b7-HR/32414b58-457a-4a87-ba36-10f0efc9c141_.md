# Nile Tech Solutions: IT and Information Security Policy

**Document owner:** IT Department
**Version:** 2.4
**Effective date:** March 1, 2026
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, interns, and visitors using company systems or networks

---

## 1. Accounts and Passwords

### 1.1 Password requirements
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, a number, and a symbol. Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The last 6 passwords cannot be reused.

### 1.2 Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, the HR portal, and all cloud systems. Employees must use the company-approved authenticator app. SMS codes are not accepted.

### 1.3 Account lockout
An account is locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed login attempts. To unlock earlier, the employee must contact the IT help desk and verify their identity.

### 1.4 Password sharing
Sharing passwords is strictly prohibited, including with managers and IT staff. IT will never ask for a password. Employees must use the company password manager to store credentials.

---

## 2. Devices

### 2.1 Company laptops
All company laptops use full-disk encryption and automatically lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Employees must not disable encryption or the screen lock.

### 2.2 Personal devices (BYOD)
Personal phones and tablets may be used only for company email and chat, and only after they are enrolled in the company Mobile Device Management (MDM) system. Personal laptops are not allowed to access internal systems.

### 2.3 Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen device must be reported to security@niletech.example within 2 hours of discovery. IT will remotely wipe the device. Failure to report within 2 hours may be treated as a policy violation.

### 2.4 Removable media
USB drives and external disks are blocked by default. Exceptions require written approval from the IT Security Manager and are limited to 30 days.

---

## 3. Network and VPN

### 3.1 VPN usage
The company VPN must be used whenever accessing internal systems from outside the office. The VPN disconnects automatically after 12 hours and the employee must log in again.

### 3.2 Public Wi-Fi
Employees must not access company systems over public Wi-Fi (cafes, airports, hotels) unless connected to the VPN.

### 3.3 Guest Wi-Fi
The guest Wi-Fi network is for visitors only. Guest credentials are generated at reception and are valid for 8 hours. Guest Wi-Fi has no access to internal systems. Employees must not use the guest network for work.

---

## 4. Data Classification and Handling

Company data is classified into four levels:

| Level | Examples | Handling rules |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Marketing material, job ads | No restrictions |
| Internal | Internal memos, org charts | Company systems only; do not share externally |
| Confidential | Contracts, financial reports | Encrypted when sent externally; share only on a need-to-know basis |
| Restricted | Client data, source code, payroll records | Company systems only; no USB, no personal email, no printing without manager approval |

### 4.1 Data retention
Email is retained for 3 years. Project files are retained for 5 years after project closure. Payroll records are retained for 10 years.

### 4.2 Sharing files
Files up to 25 MB may be sent by email. Larger files must be shared through the approved company file-sharing platform with an expiry date of no more than 30 days.

---

## 5. Email and Phishing

### 5.1 Reporting phishing
Suspicious emails must be reported using the "Report Phishing" button in the email client. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.

### 5.2 Phishing simulations
IT runs phishing simulations every quarter. An employee who fails 3 consecutive simulations must complete mandatory retraining within 14 days.

### 5.3 External senders
Emails from outside the company are tagged with an [EXTERNAL] label in the subject line.

---

## 6. Software

### 6.1 Software requests
Software installation requests are submitted through the IT portal. IT responds within 3 working days. Only software from the approved catalog may be installed without a request.

### 6.2 Open-source software
Using open-source libraries in client projects requires a license review by the Legal team before release.

### 6.3 Unlicensed software
Installing unlicensed or pirated software is prohibited and handled under the disciplinary steps in the HR Employee Handbook.

---

## 7. Security Incidents

### 7.1 Reporting
Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@niletech.example immediately. For urgent incidents outside working hours, call the security hotline at extension 9999.

### 7.2 Severity levels and response times

| Severity | Description | Initial response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 (Critical) | Data breach, ransomware, production outage | Within 1 hour |
| P2 (High) | Compromised account, malware on a single device | Within 4 hours |
| P3 (Low) | Policy violation, suspicious email with no click | Within 2 working days |

### 7.3 Breach notification
If client data is affected by a confirmed breach, the affected client must be notified within 72 hours.

---

## 8. Access Management

### 8.1 Least privilege
Employees receive only the access required for their role. Access requests must be approved by the line manager.

### 8.2 Privileged access
Administrator or production access requires approval from both the line manager and the IT Security Manager, and is reviewed every 3 months.

### 8.3 Offboarding
Access for departing employees is revoked by 5:00 PM on their last working day. Contractor access expires automatically on the contract end date.

### 8.4 Access reviews
IT performs a full access review every quarter. Managers must confirm or remove access for their team within 10 working days of receiving the review.

---

## 9. Backups

Company systems are backed up daily. Backups are kept for 30 days. Restore requests are submitted via the IT portal and are completed within 2 working days. Employees are responsible for saving work on company storage, not on local drives.

---

## 10. Security Training

All new hires must complete the information security training in their first week. All employees must complete annual refresher training by November 30 each year. Employees who miss the deadline will have their VPN access suspended until the training is completed.

---

## 11. Violations

Violations of this policy are handled under the disciplinary steps in the HR Employee Handbook: verbal warning, first written warning, final written warning, termination. Deliberate data theft or sabotage may lead to immediate termination and legal action.
